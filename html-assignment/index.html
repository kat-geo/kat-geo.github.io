About Me

Hello! I am Kat, an artist and international student at Algonquin College. I am half Greek-Cypriot and half Filipino, and have spent most of my childhood growing up in the Middle East in a British school, so my background is pretty diverse. Before taking IMD, I was in the Animation program for a bit before ultimately taking some time off to eventually decide on this current program. After my break back home, I only moved here to Canada on the 29th of August, so for the first two weeks, I was piled up with getting used to the classes and having to break my back with moving boxes... but at least now my apartment looks awesome :).

Outside of college, I have so many things that I like to do. I draw, animate, play games online, play instruments (and used to make music, though not currently), practice witchcraft/tarot reading, and much more that would make this paragraph even longer than it already is. The first thing people usually gather about me is that I am alternative, both visually and as a person - it is an integral part of my life. I have a huge love for alternative/gothic aesthetics and culture, and this includes my witchcraft work. I collect crystals, I study astrology, I practice tarot reading, and I have a passion for all kinds of divination. In regards to my art, I can get super loaded with commissions for digital illustrations or for 3D models made with Blender and Unity, and physical merchandise once in a while. I may not have any experience with web design or building, but for my small communities in art or modelling, I have my little carrd website that I share with them! Hopefully after this program, I can learn enough to be able to remake the entire thing from scratch.

Alongside my looks, most people - especially new friends - are quick to learn the importance of music in my life. Every second of the day, I am wearing earphones and listening to something (except during class because, of course, I am such a good student). As someone with ASD, having the background noise is a big comfort, and in general I just love music!
