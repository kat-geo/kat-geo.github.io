About Me

Hello! I am Kat, an artist and international student at Algonquin College. I am half Greek-Cypriot and half Filipino, and have spent most of my childhood growing up in the Middle East in a British school, so my background is pretty diverse. Before taking IMD, I was in the Animation program for a bit before ultimately deciding to take some time off to eventually decide on this current program. After my break back home, I only moved here to Canada on the 29th of August, so for the first two weeks, I was piled up with getting used to the classes and having to break my back with moving boxes (but at least now my apartment looks awesome).
